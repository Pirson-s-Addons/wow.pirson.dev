Dungeons give a lot of experience and good rewards, but their quests are spread across the world and many need a **prerequisite chain**. Go in without them and you miss half of what the dungeon gives.

## What you need to know for each dungeon

- Which quests it has and their **level**.
- Whether any needs a **prerequisite chain**, and which step you are on.
- **Who gives it** and where they are.
- What **rewards** it gives and whether they are an upgrade.
- Where the **entrance** is.

## How to have it all in game

[Dungeon Quest Atlas](/addons/dungeon-quest-atlas/) puts **over 300 WoW Forever dungeon quests** in one window styled like the Dungeon Journal:

- Every quest with its **status** (available, in progress, ready to turn in, completed or locked) and a counter per dungeon.
- The **whole chain** of each quest, including the steps outside the dungeon.
- **Rewards** with the game's own tooltip, and the **quest giver** with zone and coordinates.
- **Mark entrance on the map** and a **Route** button that takes you to the nearest flight master and then to the entrance.
- A **chat reminder** when you reach the zone of a dungeon at your level, with the quests you can still pick up.
