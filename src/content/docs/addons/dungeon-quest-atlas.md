## What it does

Dungeon Quest Atlas lists every **dungeon quest** of **WoW Forever** in a window styled like the game's own **Dungeon Journal**, together with every **boss and its loot**. Pick a dungeon and see each quest: level, faction, whether you completed it, the **whole chain**, objectives, **rewards**, the **quest giver** and where to find them. One click puts a pin on the map, on the quest giver or on the **instance entrance**, with its own guide arrow.

## Features

### Quests

- **300+ dungeon quests** with level, faction and status, using the game's own icons: **Available** (!), **In progress** (…), **Ready to turn in** (?), **Completed** (✓) or **Locked** (by level or previous quest). A "3/7 completed" counter per dungeon.
- **Quest chains**: every step with its status and a "(2/5)" in the list. Click any step, even outside the dungeon, to open it. When you turn in a step, the arrow can take you to the next one.
- **Missing quest alert**: near the entrance of a dungeon for your level, the chat lists the quests you can still pick up for it, with links; near someone who gives one, that one. Once per dungeon and quest each session; it can be turned off.
- **Objectives and rewards** in your game's language, with the game's own item tooltips (Shift to compare), choice and fixed rewards, XP and money, including the new Forever rewards.
- **Quest start and end**: the NPC or object that gives and receives the quest, with zone and coordinates, and **Mark start** / **Mark end** buttons.
- **Class quests** show their class icon, are locked for other classes and can be hidden.
- **Your progress** on the quests you carry, a **Share** button for your group and a **Wowhead link** ready to copy with Ctrl+C.

### Dungeons and bosses

- **Every dungeon** of WoW Forever, the classic ones and the new ones (Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site, City of Dalaran, The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, Shaper's Terrace), sorted by level and colored by difficulty. Scarlet Monastery, Dire Maul and Stratholme split into wings.
- **Bosses**: 3D model (drag to rotate, wheel to zoom), level, rare spawns and every drop with its chance. Shift+click links an item, Ctrl+click opens the dressing room. **Show on map** takes you to the boss.
- **Dungeon maps**, which WoW Forever doesn't have: Blizzard's WoW Classic maps for the classic dungeons and maps drawn for this addon for the new ones. Every floor, every boss marked, the entrance and the stairs, with zoom and drag.

### Loot

- **Loot roll advisor**: next to each roll, whether the item is an upgrade for you (with the % and stats), who else in the group it helps, whether a quest gives you something better and where else it drops.
- **Loot roll history** (`/dqa rolls`): what everyone chose and rolled and who won, with a private warning when someone Needs an item that is not an upgrade for them.
- **Recipes & Patterns**: the new Forever recipes of each dungeon, with the bosses that drop them and their chance.

### Maps and navigation

- **Exact instance entrance** of every dungeon, with **Mark entrance on map** and a **Route** button that takes you to the closest flight master of your faction and then to the entrance.
- **Built-in guide arrow**, no TomTom needed: it turns as you move, shows the distance, adds markers to the world map and minimap and clears itself when you arrive. Left click it to target the NPC and mark them with a star.
- **Dungeon entrances on the world map**, with their levels and your quest progress. Click one to see the dungeon map right there, or right-click to open the dungeon in the addon.

### Raids, events and your journey

- **Raids**: the announced WoW Forever raids with their size, bosses and a countdown to their opening. Onyxia's Lair includes its **attunement** chain for each faction.
- **Traveler's Log**: the **world event quests** (Hallow's End, Winter Veil, Lunar Festival, Darkmoon Faire, Midsummer...), with their dates from the game's calendar.
- **Journeys**: quests ready to hand in, dungeons for your level, the next step of your chains, your reputations in progress and what's coming soon.
- **Tutorials**: a short guide to the addon, page by page.

### Window and languages

- **Adventure Guide look**, like Retail's: tabs at the bottom, breadcrumbs to jump to any dungeon or boss, and **Overview**, **Loot**, **Model** and **Quests** pages for each dungeon.
- **Filters** (my level, classic / new, faction) and a search box for dungeons, bosses, raids and quests.
- **20 languages** with a language selector in the window, without changing your game's language.
- Minimap button (works with button collectors), window scale and Esc to close. Optional **AtlasLoot** and **Questie** support.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/dqa` | Opens or closes the window (also `/dungeonquest`) |
| `/dqa config` | Opens the options |
| `/dqa mode` | Choose Full Atlas or Adventure Mode |
| `/dqa runs` | Opens the list of your dungeon runs |
| `/dqa tracker` | Shows the dungeon run window again if you closed it |
| `/dqa rolls` | Opens the loot roll history |
| `/dqa rare` | Shows a test rare alert, to see how it looks |
| `/dqa rfk` | Opens a dungeon by its shortcut (`stocks`, `gnomer`, `dala`...) |
| `/dqa clear` | Removes all the markers |
| `/dqa bug` | Shows the address to report a bug |

### Advanced commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/dqa collect on\|off` | Collector mode (saves quest texts as you read them) |
| `/dqa export` | Shows the collected data, ready to copy |
| `/dqa entrance <key>` | Saves your position as that dungeon's entrance (`RFC`, `DM`, `WC`...) |
| `/dqa route <key> [clear]` | Saves your position as the next stop of the route to that dungeon |
| `/dqa debug` | Debug messages in the chat |

## Installation

1. Get it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/dungeon-quest-atlas) or download the zip from the [latest release](https://github.com/Pirson-s-Addons/DungeonQuestAtlas/releases/latest).
2. Extract the `DungeonQuestAtlas` folder into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`.
3. Restart WoW and enable the addon.

## FAQ

### Do I need TomTom, AtlasLoot or Questie?

No. The addon has its own arrow, map markers and loot tables. The others are optional.

### How do I use the arrow?

Drag it to move it. Left click targets the NPC and marks them with a star (out of combat). Right click removes the marker it points to. On the world map, left click a marker to be guided to it and right click to remove it.

### Why do some quests have no text?

The description is not in the addon yet. Turn on the collector (`/dqa collect on`) and open the quest in the game: the text is saved and shown at once.

### Why do some quests start with an item?

You get that item inside the dungeon (a drop or an object). "Mark start" marks the entrance.

### Why can't the addon open Wowhead?

WoW doesn't let addons open a browser or write to the clipboard. The link appears selected: press Ctrl+C.

### Does it work in combat?

Yes. It doesn't touch any protected frame.

## Compatibility

- **WoW Forever** 1.60.1. It only loads on WoW Forever: `DungeonQuestAtlas_Camelot.toc` (`Camelot` is Forever's game type) and `DungeonQuestAtlas.toc`, an identical copy the CurseForge app needs to detect the addon.
- Optional: TomTom, AtlasLoot, Questie.

## For addon authors

### Public API

Read-only, versioned, always returns copies with names in the client's language. Add `## OptionalDeps: DungeonQuestAtlas` to your TOC.

```lua
local api = DungeonQuestAtlasAPI
if api and api.version == 1 then
    api.GetDungeons()                 -- { { key, name, minLevel, maxLevel, faction, entrance = { mapID, x, y } }, ... }
    api.GetDungeon("DM")              -- one of the above
    api.FindDungeons("sm")            -- { "SM_GY", "SM_LIB", ... } from a shortcut or key
    api.GetDungeonQuests("DM")        -- { { questID, title, status, reason }, ... } for your character
    api.GetDungeonBosses("DM")        -- { { name, englishName, rare, npcID, level }, ... }
    api.GetRaids()                    -- { { key, name, entrance }, ... }
    api.GetRaid("ONYXIA")             -- { name, entrance, bosses, attunement = { itemName, steps } }
    api.GetQuest(166)                 -- { title, status, reason, level, xp, dungeonKey, rewards, choice, chain, step }
    api.GetItemSources(itemID)        -- { { who, where, chance } | { quest, where }, ... } who drops it
    api.GetQuestPlaces(questID, "starts") -- { { name, mapID, x, y }, ... } quest giver ("ends": turn-in)
    api.GetRuns()                     -- { { key, name, started, duration, finished, deaths, bosses, loot, needs, party }, ... } your runs, newest first
end
```

`status` is one of `api.STATUS`: `AVAILABLE`, `IN_LOG`, `READY`, `COMPLETED`, `BLOCKED`. The `DungeonQuestAtlas` global is the addon's internal namespace: it is not stable, use the API.

### Where the data comes from

Levels, factions, rewards, XP, bosses and loot come from the WoW Forever client (build 1.60.1, via [ForeverChanges](https://foreverchanges.pro/dungeons)); quest chains and the NPCs that start and end each quest, with their coordinates, from [Wowhead Forever](https://www.wowhead.com/forever). Dungeon entrances, quest titles, objectives and item names come from your own game, in your language. Quests the Forever client doesn't list show a small "not verified" icon. Drops and rewards can still change with game patches. Raids come from Blizzard's roadmap and the BlizzCon demo; world event quests and the new reputations, from Wowhead Forever; the Onyxia attunement, from Wowhead Classic and Wowhead Forever.

Quest descriptions are collected in the game: `/dqa collect on`, open the quests and turn them in, then `/dqa export` gives them as a Lua table. Pull requests welcome!
